Persona

An iPhone and Apple Watch assistant for the developer's personal use only, not distributed to the public or listed on the App Store.

Persona lets the developer run Claude Code on their own computer from their phone, and view and reply to their own email and work messages. When adding Gmail, authorize access with “Sign in with Google”; you do not need to provide your Google account password.

Persona is a private iPhone and Apple Watch assistant used only by its developer. It is not distributed to the public.
