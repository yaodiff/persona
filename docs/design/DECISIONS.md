# Design decisions

The history of the rules in `docs/design/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`: what was decided, when, what it replaced, and where it landed. Append only; correct an entry with a new one. This file holds no rules: where it and DESIGN_SYSTEM.md differ, DESIGN_SYSTEM.md wins. The principles and their cases are in `docs/design/PRINCIPLES.md`.

## 2026-11

- **11-24 Web's non-default looks move to Settings › Advanced › Appearance** (#13398, the Web atomic slice; iOS already has its Advanced page, Android keeps its own slice): theme color, extension theme, interface/code fonts and colored sidebar icons leave General for a new **Advanced** category, the last row under This device. They are the same components reading the same browser storage keys, so a choice made before the move still applies and the untouched default look is pixel-identical; nothing is retired and no token or baseline changes. General keeps the default appearance (Match system / Light / Dark), text size, language and the behavior rows. Rejected: moving Match system / Light / Dark or text size as well (the default appearance and content scaling are not non-default styles); retiring any little-used option here (each retirement needs the maintainer's decision in #13398). Where: `docs/web/web.md` "Settings" and "Theme color and text size"; `web/src/views/settings/nav.ts`, the `Advanced` page in `web/src/views/Settings.tsx`.

- **11-24 New Agent lists a launcher whose check failed as unavailable, with why, instead of leaving it out** (#14535, the owner's call after an Antigravity launcher vanished from the picker with no word of why): the hub's New Agent list puts a launcher whose latest check failed on that computer in `unavailable` (code `launcher_unhealthy`) with the failure's class and the record's line, and every client draws it in its existing unavailable group (grayed, never picked, How to fix opens the provider's page). Rejected: listing it pickable with Try Anyway again (#1643's rule, which 11-11 replaced for a reason that still holds). Where: `docs/server/launcher-health.md` "In the App".

- **11-23 The To-dos list's search finds closed to-dos, on the device only** (#10465's search step; iOS, hubs with `todo_view`, from #10465's switch-on): the list's own field looks through the titles and notes of every to-do of the current assistant the phone holds, open and closed, and lists what closed last, by the days the Filter's Done lists, each heading naming Done; the place and the Filter's choice wait until the field is cleared, the headings count matches and the drawer's N does not move. A to-do found by its notes says the matched sentence as its line 2, the words on the accent's soft ground as "List controls" and the web's chat search mark them, not the list design's bold (principle 4). iOS's chat search and Knowledge mark their found words the same way since #14401 (#14320). Results keep their tick but offer no Mark Done and no answers. Why the device only: `GET /api/v1/todos` returns every to-do and the phone keeps them, so a hub pass over titles and notes could find nothing the phone lacks, at the cost of a feature flag, a fan-out across managers and fallbacks for older hubs. Replaces: "Closed to-dos and the hub's own matches come with the search's next step" (11-21); the hub's merge stays a later step, for what the phone does not hold (a to-do's rounds, results and recap). Rejected: the app's full search opened on a To-dos chip, which an earlier plan for this step had, before the owner chose the list's own field (11-21). Where: 3.4 "To-dos list" and "List controls", 3.5 "To-dos places"; `docs/apple/ios.md` "To-dos (V1, #10465)"; `docs/assistant/todos.md`.

- **11-23 Persona chats under Pinned and Recent show Unread on the web, and their folded headings count it** (#13425, web; the issue's proposal, matching the iOS and Android drawers): a chat with a reply nobody has opened draws the unread `StatusMark` in its chat icon's slot (Just finished its plain dot) with the Agent rows' unread title weight, cleared the moment it is opened through the seen outbox.
